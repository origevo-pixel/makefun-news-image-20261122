THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — a one-picture front page of Sunday 22 November 2026. Five editorial panels with typeset captions cover the day's biggest stories: Iran strikes Bahrain and tankers are hit in Hormuz, Mexico captures cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro', COP31 collapses in Antalya with no deal after the EU rejects a text silent on fossil fuels, a Russian warship is sunk by sea drones at Benghazi, Libya, and Wall Street falls into correction in an AI sell-off — plus an ALSO TODAY strip with the EU–Indonesia trade deal, the Kryvyi Rih strike, the Democrats' record House majority (258 seats) and Alcaraz's ATP Finals title. The panels were generated as text-free illustrations and every word was typeset programmatically (build/compose.py), so the date and headlines stay crisp. main.ts hands the finished 1024×1024 picture in as entry.png.
